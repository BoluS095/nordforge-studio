1. The 3 Highest-Impact Fixes You Can Apply in 15 Minutes (Free)

  1. Remove Orphaned App Embeds in theme.liquid: 64% of stores load 35+ external scripts. Uninstalling a Shopify app from Admin does not always remove its legacy <script> tags from your theme files.
  2. Fix Missing Organization/Product JSON-LD Schema: 26% of audited storefronts expose zero application/ld+json tags on entry, hurting Google Shopping rich price/stock snippets during Black Friday search spikes.
  3. Lock Mobile 390px Horizontal Overflow & Broken Media: 17% of stores rendered broken <img> tags (naturalWidth === 0). Adding html, body { max-width: 100%; overflow-x: clip; } prevents horizontal jitter on iOS/Android checkout scrolls.
